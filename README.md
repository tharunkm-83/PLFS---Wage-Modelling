# PLFS---Wage-Modelling
#### Introduction
The objectives of this assessment was to familiarize ourselves with the regression models,
which has significant applications in the domains of economics. This was achieved by de
veloping many linear regression models on the National Sample Survey’s (NSS) Periodic
Labour Force Survey (PLFS) July 2023- June 2024 data. We sought to test the Life-Cycle
Hypothesis and Gary Becker’s Hypothesis on Diminishing Returns to Higher Education. We
also tried to determine the role of Gender and the Rural/ Urban status of the place where
the individual resides on the wages a person earns in the Indian labour force. Some of the
key explanatory variables we looked at include:
1. Years of Formal Education
2. Years of Formal Education-Squared
3. Age (as a proxy for Experience)
4. Age-Squared
5. Sector (Urban/Rural)
6. Gender (Female/Male
7. Sector X Gender (Interaction term)

#### Methodology
This study employs an econometric approach to analyze the determinants of wages in India
using the Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) data for the period July 2023–June 2024. A
multivariate linear regression model is estimated to examine the relationship between wages
and various socio-demographic and labor market characteristics. The analysis is conducted
using Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) regression
